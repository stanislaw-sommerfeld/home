01 About

I work on the dynamics and control of autonomous systems, from drones to spacecraft, and on the physics and numerical methods beneath them.

My training ran through a French classe préparatoire, a year in the United States in aerospace and modern physics, and a specialization in materials and processes before the move into autonomous robotics. The projects here turn that background into working, reproducible code.

02 Research

Space engineering & autonomy

Orbital mechanics, guidance, navigation and control, and autonomous space robotics. From a clean two-body orbit to holding a satellite's attitude under noisy sensors and an uncertain model.

Theoretical & computational physics

Classical and Hamiltonian mechanics, modern physics, and the numerical methods that make them computable: tracking invariants, checking convergence, and treating every simulation as a model.
