P0 · Primary lab research

SkyJEPA

In progress

Reproduction of SkyJEPA for autonomous flight: 6-DoF dynamics, world models, and predictive control. The aim is a faithful, diagnosable reproduction before any claim of a new result.

Mastery goals

  • Physics — 6-DoF dynamics, rotation on SO(3), forces, moments, mass and inertia parameters, perturbations and simulation assumptions.
  • Control — geometric and integral control, MPPI, cost function, stability, steady-state error, saturation, and the performance/cost trade-off.
  • Learning — latent representation, action-conditioned prediction, horizon, generalization, and the limits of a world model.

Validation milestones

  • A. Reproduction — report conditions, versions, seeds, baselines, metrics and gaps to reference results.
  • B. Diagnosis — explain observations through the dynamics; targeted ablations on the integral action.
  • C. Robustness — compare methods at equal budget, same initial conditions and perturbations, multiple seeds, with uncertainty intervals.
  • D. Transmission — reproducible README, a central figure, a 3–5 page report, and a five-minute talk. Negative results kept.

Repository and project page to be linked once published.

P1 · Computational Astrodynamics Lab

OrbitLab

Planned MVP ~30–45 h

How faithfully can gravitational dynamics be integrated, and what does accuracy cost in conservation of invariants and compute time? An educational project first.

Technical path

  • M1 · Two-body — derive Newtonian equations and Kepler's laws; SI units and non-dimensionalization; circular and elliptical orbits against an analytical reference.
  • M2 · Numerical methods — RK4 and velocity Verlet; compare convergence, energy and angular-momentum error, runtime and long-term stability.
  • M3 · Three-body — restricted circular problem, rotating frame, Jacobi constant; Lagrange points and trajectories, checking the right invariants.
  • M4 · Optional extension — transfer trajectories and differential correction, then the DMD/EDMD/Koopman literature. No neural network before the physics baselines hold.

MVP completion criteria

M1–M2 required: circular-orbit tests, time-step convergence, comparison to a reference, invariant-drift figures over a fixed horizon, and a documented reproduction command. M3–M4 are extensions.
