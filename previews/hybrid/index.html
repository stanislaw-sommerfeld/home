01 / About

I work on the dynamics and control of autonomous systems, from drones to spacecraft, and on the physics and numerical methods beneath them.

My training ran through a French classe préparatoire, a year in the United States in aerospace and modern physics, and a specialization in materials and processes before the move into autonomous robotics. The projects here turn that background into working, reproducible code.

SkyJEPA

In progress

Reproduction of SkyJEPA for autonomous flight at SNU: 6-DoF dynamics, world models, predictive control. A faithful, diagnosable reproduction with documented robustness tests.

Planned

Computational astrodynamics: how faithfully can gravitational dynamics be integrated, and what does accuracy cost in conservation and compute? Two-body, then restricted three-body; RK4 against symplectic integrators.
